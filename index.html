−300m / faq

 
Asked in the deep.

 

  
What is CLAM?
A Solana launchpad where every coin fills sealed, then deploys, reveals and opens its pool in one step.

  
What can I see before I buy?
The hint, raise, deadline, cap, fill and the public seal. Not the name, ticker or art.

  
Why buy what you can't see?
Everyone buys the same thing, on the same terms. The hint is the pitch. The reveal is the moment.

  
How are snipers stopped?
The token doesn't exist while it fills. It's created and pooled in the same transaction.

  
How are rugs stopped?
LP locked at open. Mint and freeze revoked. No dev bag.

  
How are insiders stopped?
No whitelist, no team allocation, one price, 2% cap. The creator included.

  
How are copycats stopped?
Nobody can copy a ticker they haven't seen. By the reveal, the real one is live.

  
How big is a raise?
10 to 100 SOL, set at filing. It opens the moment it's full.

  
Why one price?
Everyone inside pays the price the pool opens at. First in and last in are equal.

  
Can I pull out before it fills?
Yes. Any time before it fills, you take back what you put in.

  
Can the creator swap the name?
No. A different name gives a different fingerprint. Check it in the console.

  
Why the salt?
Tickers are short and guessable. 32 random bytes make guessing impossible.

  
What if it never fills?
It never opens. Everyone takes back exactly what they put in.

  
What does $CLAM do?
Every clam that opens pays a fee that buys back and burns $CLAM.

  
Is it live?
The seal and verify tools are. The escrow program ships next.

  
Where is the CA?
Sealed. Only this site and the CLAM account post it.
